\chapter{Integrated System and Discussion}\label{ch08}

\Cref{ch04,ch05,ch06,ch07} described and evaluated the builder and the query engine separately. This chapter
looks at them together: how the system is operated end to end (\cref{sec:ch08-end-to-end-operation}), one question
traced through it (\cref{sec:ch08-a-worked-example}), which part of the system does which work
(\cref{sec:ch08-what-the-graph-does-and-what-s}), the trade-offs the design makes
(\cref{sec:ch08-trade-offs}), how general it is (\cref{sec:ch08-generality}), and the threats to the validity of the
results (\cref{sec:ch08-threats-to-validity}).

\section{End-to-End Operation}\label{sec:ch08-end-to-end-operation}

\paragraph{Building.} A graph is built by running the builder's stages over a data folder, with a goal stated in one
sentence: \texttt{kg run data/ --goal "..."} runs them all, and each stage can also be run and inspected alone
(\cref{tab:builder-stages}). The plan and the text schema are written as files that a person may review before the
build continues. A build of one of the thesis datasets costs well under two dollars (\cref{sec:ch05-cost}); a rebuild
from the response cache costs nothing and reproduces the same graph, which a content digest confirms.

\paragraph{Asking.} Questions are asked from the command line, one at a time or as a set scored against gold
answers, or through a web interface. The interface (\texttt{kg serve}) is a small web server and a single-page
application. It shows one tab per dataset, with the graph's records, documents and their links drawn as a network, and
a question panel. While the agent works, each model reply, tool call and send-back is streamed to the page as it
happens, with the tool's running time and the model's tokens and cost. The answer shows its outcome, its time and
cost, and numbered source markers; a marker opens the cited passage with its quotation, or the cited query with its
statement and rows, and rings the cited records on the graph. The page can also reload a dataset's graph into the
database from the cache, for no model cost, under the same lock that prevents two questions from running at once.

\paragraph{One graph at a time.} The working database holds one graph. The server asks a question only of the dataset
whose graph is loaded, which it recognises by the graph's digest; another dataset must be reloaded first.

\section{What the Graph Does and What SQL and the Agent Do}\label{sec:ch08-what-the-graph-does-and-what-s}

A system that combines a graph, text search, typed queries and a language model invites attributing its accuracy to
the graph. The measurements do not support that attribution without qualification.

\paragraph{How the agent uses the tools.} Over the 440 unseen questions of the four datasets, the agent made 1{,}244
tool calls (\cref{tab:tool-usage}). A third went to graph tools, a third to text search, and a third to the schema and
typed queries. Two figures stand out. The agent queried the source tables (179 calls) more than twice as often as the
graph's records (71), although both hold the same rows. And it searched the stored claims once in 440 questions: the
claim layer that the first representations were built around is, for the agent, almost unused.

\paragraph{What each part contributes.} Read together with the evaluation chapters, the evidence supports a
division of labour rather than a single source of accuracy:
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
\item \emph{Exact answers} --- counts, filters, joins --- come from the typed queries, executed by DuckDB or by
Cypher over the record layer. This matches the earlier finding that the records did the exact work
(\cref{sec:ch04-evolution}), and it is where the agent gains most over retrieval-only readers
(\cref{sec:ch07-baselines}).
\item \emph{Finding} what a question is about comes from the node cards (\texttt{find\_nodes}, a seventh of all
calls) and from ranked text search.
\item \emph{Evidence} comes from the text: passages found by ranked or exhaustive search, read whole by the model.
\item \emph{Identity and linking} --- the part of the graph that \cref{ch05} shows to be exact --- reach the agent
indirectly: through the cards, which are built from the identity layer; through \texttt{node\_evidence} and
\texttt{neighbours}, which walk it; and through the record fields \texttt{get\_node} shows with their sources.
\item \emph{Judgement} --- reading, choosing the next step, deciding the outcome --- is the model's, and is where most
remaining errors lie (\cref{sec:ch07-errors}).
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{What has not been measured.} The engine was never run without its graph tools on the same questions, so
the share of its accuracy that the graph provides is not known. The baselines of \cref{sec:ch07-baselines} lack the
graph \emph{and} the typed queries \emph{and} the agent loop at once. The thesis's claims about the graph therefore
rest on the builder's audit --- the graph's identity, links and provenance are correct --- and not on a measured
contribution to answer accuracy. An ablation of the graph tools is the most informative experiment this design still
lacks (\cref{ch09}).

\section{Trade-offs}\label{sec:ch08-trade-offs}

\paragraph{Precision against recall in the builder.} The builder stores few wrong facts but misses many (96--99\,\%
precision, 18--58\,\% recall of a reader's claims), and its identity decisions err towards keeping things apart. The
design accepts the low recall because the query engine does not rely on stored facts: it reads the text.

\paragraph{An index against a fact store.} The anchor graph gives up answering from stored facts in exchange for
links and identity that can be checked. The price is that every answer about what the text says needs a model to read
it, which costs time and money at query time instead of build time.

\paragraph{Determinism against flexibility.} Typed queries are validated and compiled by code and cannot write, but
only express what their language allows; about half of the fixes between the confirmation sets extended that
language. Free SQL
is more expressive and answered more of the targeted failures, but moves trust from the compiler to a parser check.

\paragraph{Cost and latency against accuracy.} The agent costs about 1.6--2.2 cents and 10--12 seconds per question, against
0.1 cent and 2--7 seconds for a retrieval-only reader, and answers far more questions. Medium thinking answers more of
the hardest questions at about three times the cost and latency.

\section{Generality}\label{sec:ch08-generality}

\paragraph{By topic.} No code, setting or prompt names a domain; the four datasets cover furniture, vehicle safety,
aviation accidents and a mixed corpus of a utility, an institute and local news, and the same code built and queried
all of them. The one dataset unseen during development passed every hard audit gate and every benchmark question.

\paragraph{By form.} All four datasets share one form: small corpora of short documents, each mostly about one or a
few things, with names written in full and tables of at most a few hundred rows. The identity model assumes that a
mention means one thing within a document, which already failed on reports that describe several accidents. Long
narratives, scientific documents with defined abbreviations, and large tables are untested. The thesis therefore
claims generality across topics within this form, not across forms.

The first threat deserves emphasis. The reference answers, the judgements by meaning and much of the failure analysis
were produced by one model family (Claude), while the system under test uses another (Gemini). This independence
between judge and system is deliberate and avoids self-preference~\cite{zheng2023judging}; it does not remove the
possibility that the gold writer and the judge share errors. A sample of verdicts checked by a human would give an
agreement figure; none was made.

\section{Ethical and Practical Considerations}\label{sec:ch08-ethics}

\paragraph{Data.} The NHTSA and NTSB records are public works of the US government; personal details the NTSB
export contains (owners' and operators' addresses, crew home towns, private operators' names) were removed before
use. The furniture and generality data are synthetic.

\paragraph{Safety of querying.} The engine cannot change the data: requests to do so are refused before any model
call, and every database connection is read-only. A user can still ask questions whose answers expose what the data
holds; access control to the data itself is outside the system.

\paragraph{Dependence on a model provider.} Building and answering depend on a commercial model service. Costs are
low and logged, and the cache makes past results reproducible without it, but a new build or question needs the
service, and model versions change.

\paragraph{Generated reference data.} Because the gold was written by a language model, the thesis states the
provenance of every judged number and does not present judged scores as ground truth.
