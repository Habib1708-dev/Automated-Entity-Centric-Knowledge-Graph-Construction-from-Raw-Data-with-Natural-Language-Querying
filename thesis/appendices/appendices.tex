This appendix lists every measurement of the dataset excluded from the results (\cref{sec:ch03-datasets}),
measured with the same code, rules and judges as the four reported datasets.

\paragraph{The dataset.} Ten encyclopaedia articles (5{,}729 words) about lighthouses, with three tables of
open structured data about the same lighthouses: 80 lighthouses, 62 events and 4 engineers. It was built on
12~October~2026 for \$0.689.
