\paragraph{Use of AI tools.} Large language models are part of the system this thesis studies: Gemini models build the
graphs and answer the questions, and Claude models wrote the reference answers and judged results by meaning, as
stated in \cref{sec:ch03-ground-truth,sec:ch03-scoring-exact-match-and-judgement}. In addition, an AI coding and
writing assistant (Claude, through Claude Code) assisted in implementing the software, analysing the recorded results,
drawing the figures and drafting the text of this thesis. I directed this work, reviewed its output, and take
responsibility for the content.
